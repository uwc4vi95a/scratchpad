# STRIDE Cheat Sheet

Quick reference for threat modeling.

## Spoofing
- Authenticate identities
- Use strong authN

## Tampering
- Integrity checks
- Sign data

## Repudiation
- Audit logs
- Non-repudiation

## Information Disclosure
- Encrypt data
- Least privilege

## Denial of Service
- Rate limiting
- Redundancy

## Elevation of Privilege
- Principle of least privilege
- Sandboxing

## Useful Questions
- What are the entry points?
- What are the trust boundaries?
- What data is sensitive?
