# Threat Modeling Checklist

Quick prompts for security reviews and scratchpad notes.

## Assets
- What are we protecting?
- Data sensitivity / classification
- Trust boundaries

## Attack surfaces
- Entry points accessible to untrusted users
- AuthN/AuthZ gaps
- Third-party integrations

## Threats
- Spoofing, tampering, repudiation, information disclosure, DoS, elevation (STRIDE)
- Realistic adversary profiles
- Abuse cases

## Mitigations
- Controls already in place
- Gaps and acceptance
- Test/validation plan

## Follow-up
- Owner and deadline
- Links to relevant code/docs
